# A New Dream

A playable 3D browser adventure with stylized Rapunzel, Mother Gothel, Eugene, a divine cat, and a white horse.

Escape the house with a frying pan, aim at five bandits from horseback, take the reins up the castle ramp, and choose how to answer the proposal. Both choices finish with the closing curtain.

## Play

Open [A New Dream](https://just-poet.github.io/a-new-dream/) in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari with WebGL 2 support.

The game also runs offline by opening `dist/index.html`.

## Controls

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**.
- Swing the pan with **Space**, a click in the scene, or the touch button.
- Hold **Shift** or tap **Dodge** to avoid Gothel.
- Click or tap the marked bandits to shoot.
- Hold **W / Up** to ride the horse. Use **A / D** or **Left / Right** to steer, and **S / Down** to slow down.
- Press **Esc** to pause. Use the music button to mute or enable sound.

## Build

From `Source`, run `npm ci` and then `npm run build`. The build produces the standalone `dist/index.html`.

All characters and environments are built from 3D geometry. The visual references informed their clothing, hair, faces, and setting.
